Engineered for Rigorous Geospatial Demands

Built for GIS analysts, surveyors, cartographers, and defense teams requiring uncompromising precision.

Universal Vector Engine

Seamless conversions across OGC GeoPackage (.gpkg), ESRI Shapefile suite (.shp, .shx, .dbf, .prj), GeoJSON RFC 7946, Google Earth KML, and Spatial CSV.

PROJ 6,900+ EPSG Systems

Rigorous mathematical datum shifts (WGS 84, Lambert-93, UTM, Web Mercator, British National Grid) with explicit distinction between Reproject and Assign CRS.

Spatial Doctor & Auto-Repair

Automated heuristic diagnosis for inverted Lat/Lon coordinates ($X \leftrightarrow Y$) with 1-click swap repair, polygon ring snapping, and duplicate vertex cleaning.

Surface Geodesy & Aggregations

Seamlessly append geodesic surface area columns (surface_ha, surface_m2) and build dynamic group-by statistics.

Multi-Layer GPKG & QGIS Handoff

Consolidate an entire directory of mixed vector files into a single unified OGC GeoPackage container accompanied by an auto-generated styled QGIS 3.x project (.qgs).

Certified QA/QC Audit Records

Cryptographically sealed compliance records with SHA-256 hashes, exact PROJ mathematical parameter disclosure, and exportable printable audit certificates.
